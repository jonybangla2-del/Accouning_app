  • House Rent: 50% of Basic (Bangladesh corporate standard)
  • Provident Fund: 10% Employee Deduction, 2.5% Employer Contribution
  • Loyalty Allowance: 15% of Basic for ≥10 yrs service; 7.5% for >5 yrs service
  • Disbursal Channels: EFT (Premier Bank), BEFTN (Other Banks), Cash
