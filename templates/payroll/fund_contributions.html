This automated engine will scan all employee contribution records across all months and perform:

  • 1. Swap Fix: Automatically swaps inverted values where Company Share is greater than Employee Share (e.g. May–Dec 2022).
  • 2. Zero EMP Fix: Restores missing 0 Employee Contribution entries (e.g. Mar-2023) based on the employee's surrounding regular salary rate.
  • 3. Cumulative Balance Recalculation: Sequentially recalculates balance_emp, balance_com, and total_balance from month 1 to the end for all employees.
